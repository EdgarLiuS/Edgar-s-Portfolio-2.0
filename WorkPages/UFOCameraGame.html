UFO Camera Game

Capture a UFO with hand gestures!

About This Work

This is one of my latest P5 projects. By utilizing the ml5 hand-tracking AI (machine learning) library, I was able to use a camera to recognize a player's hand gestures and design an experimental game based on those movements.

Players must make an upward-pointing index finger gesture (☝️) to control the in-game camera; they must then aim the camera at a constantly dodging UFO to take a rapid burst of photos. Once a specific number of shots has been successfully captured, the player wins.

Furthermore, the movement patterns of the UFO in this game are inspired by Lévy flights, generating unpredictable, random motions that add a significant layer of challenge to the gameplay.

Keywords: Game-design, AI, Hand-tracking, Interaction design, Algorithmic application
